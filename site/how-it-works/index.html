How it works · Chord OTC

Approve, fill, settled.

How a trade moves wallet to wallet on Arc, how the contract is tested, and straight answers to the usual questions. Ready to trade? Open the desk.

How it works

01Approve

The maker approves USDC or a token and names the exact price, optionally for one named taker. Nothing leaves their wallet.

02Fill

A taker pays the other side. Nothing moves unless both legs move, so neither party has to trust the other.

03Settled

Both sides are paid in the same transaction. The fee is shown on the desk before you sign and is fixed when the offer is made (0% during the zero-fee beta promo, until 31 Dec 2026). Unfilled offers can be cancelled any time; nothing was ever taken, so there is nothing to refund.

The contract never holds funds. If either side can't be paid (the maker moved their funds, or a wallet is on the USDC blocklist), the fill simply fails and nothing moves. Offers that can't fill right now are greyed out. A greyed-out offer is not cancelled: it becomes fillable again at its old price if the maker's balance and approval come back, so cancel offers you no longer want.

Questions

OTC on Arc, in plain words

What is Chord OTC?

A peer-to-peer OTC desk on Circle's Arc network. A maker names an exact price for a token against USDC, and a taker fills it. Both sides move wallet to wallet in one transaction, so there is no order book, no slippage and no middleman holding funds. Anyone with an Arc wallet can use it, except US and UK persons; there is no sign-up.

Does Chord hold my funds?

No. Your side stays in your own wallet, covered by an ordinary approval, until someone fills your offer. The contract never holds a balance between trades, and you can cancel an open offer at any time.

Which tokens can I trade?

USDC against EURC, GBPA, MXNB, WETH, cirBTC, LINK, VIRTUAL, ARGUS and ARCUS on Arc mainnet, plus other ERC-20 tokens by address, with a code check before you trade.

What does it cost?

Nothing during the beta: every offer made before 31 December 2026 settles fee-free. After that the fee is a flat 0.25%, and you pay Arc gas in USDC, usually a fraction of a cent.

Can I trade with one specific person?

Yes. Set a private taker address when you make the offer and only that wallet can fill it, which suits pre-agreed block trades between two parties.

Is Chord audited?

Not yet. The contract is open source and verified, fuzz- and fork-tested, reviewed in-house and by Proovant, and pre-scanned by Olympix. Until an independent audit, offers are capped at about 1,000 USDC each.
