How it works

A maker locks one side of the trade in the escrow and names the price. Anyone (or one named taker) fills it by paying the other side; both legs settle in a single transaction. The maker can cancel an unfilled offer at any time for a full refund. A 0.25% fee is taken from the USDC leg. If a payout can't be delivered (for example a USDC-blocklisted wallet), it is held here for that wallet to claim instead of blocking the trade.
