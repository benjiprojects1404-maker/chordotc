How it works

01Lock

The maker locks USDC or a token in the escrow contract and names the exact price, optionally for one named taker.

02Fill

A taker pays the other side. Nothing moves unless both legs move, so neither party has to trust the other.

03Settled

Both sides are paid in the same transaction. A 0.25% fee comes off the USDC leg. Unfilled offers can be cancelled any time for a full refund.

If a payout can't be delivered (for example to a USDC-blocklisted wallet), it is held for that wallet to claim instead of blocking the trade.
