Try it free · Arc Testnet

Your first escrowed trade in five minutes.

Chord OTC locks one side of a trade in escrow and settles both sides in a single transaction. The testnet desk works the same way as the live one, but with free test tokens, so you can try every step without risking a cent.

Open the testnet desk Get free test USDC ↗

  1. Have a wallet ready

    MetaMask, Rabby or any browser wallet works. A fresh account is a good idea for testing.

  2. Get free test USDC

    Go to faucet.circle.com, choose Arc Testnet and paste your address. On Arc, USDC also pays the network fees, so this one faucet covers everything.

  3. Open the testnet desk and connect

    Open chordotc.com/?net=testnet and press Connect. The site adds Arc Testnet to your wallet for you.

  4. Make a trade

    Fill an offer: pick one from Open offers, pay the test USDC and the tokens arrive in the same transaction.

    Or make your own: press Mint 100 test CTT, then create an offer to sell them for USDC, or one that pays USDC for them. You can't fill your own offer, so send the link to a friend or fill it from a second wallet account.

  5. Tell us how it went

    Reply to @chordotc with a screenshot or your transaction link, or email benjamin@chordotc.com. Anything confusing is worth hearing about.

Test tokens only. Testnet USDC and CTT have no value and can't be moved to mainnet. Chord OTC is unaudited beta software; the live desk on Arc mainnet has per-offer limits.

Questions

Is this real money?
No. Everything on Arc Testnet uses free test tokens.
What is CTT?
Chord Test Token, a token that only exists on testnet so you have something to trade against USDC. Anyone can mint 100 from the desk.
What happens to my tokens if nobody fills my offer?
Cancel it at any time from My offers and the escrowed tokens come straight back to you.
Where's the code?
The contract, tests and site are open source on GitHub.
