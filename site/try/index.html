Open testnet desk
Try it free · Arc Testnet

Your first OTC trade in five minutes.

Chord OTC settles both sides of a trade wallet to wallet in a single transaction, and never holds your funds in between. The testnet desk works the same way as the live one, but with free test tokens, so you can try every step without risking a cent.

Open the testnet desk Get free test USDC ↗

  1. Have a wallet ready

    MetaMask, Rabby or any browser wallet works. A fresh account is a good idea for testing.

  2. Get free test USDC

    Go to faucet.circle.com, choose Arc Testnet and paste your address. Take USDC (it also pays the network fees on Arc), and EURC too if you'd like to sell some.

  3. Open the testnet desk and connect

    Open chordotc.com/?net=testnet and press Connect. The site adds Arc Testnet to your wallet for you.

  4. Make a trade

    Fill an offer: pick one from Open offers, pay the test USDC and the tokens arrive in the same transaction.

    Or make your own: pick EURC on the create form and set a price close to the market rate the desk shows. You can't fill your own offer, so send the link to a friend or fill it from a second wallet account. (No EURC? Pick CTT and press Mint 100 test CTT to get a test token straight from the desk.)

  5. Tell us how it went

    Reply to @chordotc with a screenshot or your transaction link, or email benjamin@chordotc.com. Anything confusing is worth hearing about.

Test tokens only. Testnet USDC, EURC and CTT have no value and can't be moved to mainnet. Chord OTC is unaudited beta software; the live desk on Arc mainnet has per-offer limits.

Questions

Is this real money?
No. Everything on Arc Testnet uses free test tokens.
What is CTT?
Chord Test Token, a token that only exists on testnet so you have something to trade against USDC. Anyone can mint 100 from the desk.
What happens to my tokens if nobody fills my offer?
They never left your wallet: the offer only has your approval. Cancel it at any time from My offers, or simply revoke the approval.
Where's the code?
The contract, tests and site are open source on GitHub.
