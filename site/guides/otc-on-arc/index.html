How to do an OTC trade on Arc

Updated 27 November 2026 · about a 4 minute read

An OTC trade on Arc takes about five minutes: one side names an exact price, the other fills it, and both legs settle wallet to wallet in a single transaction. This guide walks through it on Chord OTC, a peer-to-peer desk on Circle's Arc network.

OTC (over the counter) means trading directly with a counterparty at an agreed price, instead of selling into a pool or an order book. It is how desks move size without slippage: if you agree 1,000 EURC for 1,080 USDC, that is exactly what each side receives, however thin the pools are that day.

Arc suits this well. Gas is paid in USDC, fees are usually a fraction of a cent, and the main assets (USDC, EURC, WETH, cirBTC) are the ones people actually want to swap in blocks.

What you need

A browser wallet and a little USDC on Arc mainnet are all it takes.

  • A wallet. MetaMask, Rabby or any browser wallet. When you press Connect on chordotc.com, the site adds Arc (chain ID 5042) to your wallet for you.
  • USDC for gas. Arc uses USDC as its gas token, so keep about 0.10 USDC spare on top of what you trade.
  • The asset you want to give. If you are selling a token for USDC, hold the token. If you are buying, hold the USDC.
  • No USDC on Arc yet? Bridge it from another chain with Circle's CCTP. Or, when someone sends you a Crescent payment link, buy it by bank transfer from a US or EU bank right on the page, delivered straight to your own wallet.

Making an offer

As the maker you name the price, and your side never leaves your wallet until someone fills it.

  1. Connect

    Open chordotc.com and press Connect wallet.

  2. Choose a side

    In Create offer, choose I give USDC (you are buying a token) or I give token (you are selling one).

  3. Pick the token

    Listed tokens include EURC, GBPA, MXNB, WETH, cirBTC, LINK, VIRTUAL, ARGUS and ARCUS. Any other ERC-20 can be pasted by address, and the desk runs a code check on it first.

  4. Set the amounts

    Enter what you give and what you want. The form shows your price next to the current market rate, the fee and exactly what you will receive.

  5. Choose an expiry

    1 hour, 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, or never (you cancel it yourself).

  6. Approve

    Press 1 · Approve. This lets the contract move your side only if the offer is filled. Nothing is transferred.

  7. Create the offer

    Press 2 · Create offer and confirm. The offer appears in Open offers, with a link you can share with a counterparty.

Filling an offer

As the taker you pay one side, and the other side arrives in the same transaction or nothing moves at all.

  1. Open the offer

    Pick one from Open offers, or open the link the maker sent you.

  2. Check it

    Check the price, the token address and the expiry. For a token you don't know, read the code check shown beside it.

  3. Approve

    Press Approve so the contract can take your side of this one trade.

  4. Fill

    Press Fill and confirm. Both legs settle in one transaction: you get the maker's side, the maker gets yours, and the receipt is on the Arc explorer.

If the maker has moved their funds or withdrawn their approval since posting, the desk marks the offer as not fillable, and a fill attempt simply fails without taking anything from you.

Private offers, cancelling, fees and limits

  • Private offers. Under Options, set a private taker address and only that wallet can fill. This is the way to settle a pre-agreed block trade with one counterparty.
  • Cancelling. Cancel an open offer any time from My offers. Because your funds never left your wallet, there is nothing to withdraw; revoking the approval also stops it.
  • Fees. Fee-free until 31 December 2026, then a flat 0.25% on the USDC leg. Gas is paid in USDC and is usually a fraction of a cent.
  • Limits. Chord is in mainnet beta and not independently audited yet, so each offer is capped at about 1,000 USDC (1,000 EURC, 0.5 WETH and similar).
  • Who can use it. Anyone with an Arc wallet, except US and UK persons. There is no sign-up.

Try it on testnet first

The testnet desk works exactly like the live one with free test tokens, so you can run every step above without risking anything.

  1. Get test tokens

    Get free test USDC (and EURC if you want to sell some) from faucet.circle.com, choosing Arc Testnet.

  2. Connect

    Open chordotc.com/?net=testnet and connect.

  3. Trade

    Fill an open offer, or make your own and fill it from a second wallet account. No EURC? Pick CTT and press Mint 100 test CTT.

Before you trade for real

  • Only trade with people you know, and confirm the token address with them another way. A look-alike token can copy any name.
  • Start small. Blockchain transactions can't be reversed.
  • Read how Chord is tested: the security review covers the in-house review, the Olympix pre-audit scan and Proovant's findings, and the contract and tests are open source.
