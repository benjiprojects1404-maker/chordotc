# Chord OTC v2: funds are never held

`contracts/ChordOTCv2.sol` replaces the v1 escrow with offers that **never hold funds**, the same design as
Handshake v4 on BlockDAG.

**Deployed on Arc mainnet (19 Nov 2026):** `0x41a2bb48a9271C7Dc4cf3E36D70F497aBf2878e8`, from Chord_Deployer.
Verified on explorer.arc.io (partial match: Remix stored the file under another path, which only changes the
metadata hash). Fee recipient set to the Safe, fee 0 (promo), the 8 beta caps set. Ownership accepted by the Safe
(Safe nonce 4). v1 paused for new offers (Safe nonce 6; nonce 5 was an on-chain rejection of a mis-built batch).
Not deployed on testnet: the testnet desk stays on the v1 testnet contract for now.

**Smoke test passed (19 Nov 2026, 12:10):** offer #1, Chord_Deployer gave 0.1 EURC for 0.11 USDC; Chord_Taker filled it
(tx `0xede82d08ef3b564650ab1a62121b2213f3170c28082329ec549ec7cb26a53af3`). Two transfers, both wallet to wallet:
0.11 USDC taker → deployer and 0.1 EURC deployer → taker. The contract was never in the path; fee 0 during the promo.

## What changes for users

| | v1 (live) | v2 |
|---|---|---|
| Making an offer | Approve, then the contract takes your USDC or token | Approve only; it stays in your wallet |
| Filling | Taker pays in; contract pays out | Both sides move wallet to wallet in one transaction |
| Cancelling | Contract refunds you | Marks the offer cancelled; nothing to refund (revoking the approval also stops fills) |
| Maker moves their funds | Not possible (locked) | The fill fails and nothing moves; the site greys the offer out (`offerReady(id)`) |
| USDC-blocklisted wallet | Payout held as a credit to claim later | The fill fails and nothing moves; no credits, no `withdraw()` |
| Pause | Stops new offers | Stops new offers **and fills** (safe now: makers keep their funds). Cancelling always works |

Kept the same: function names and arguments, the `Offer` struct and events (so the site barely changed), the
fee (0.25% default, max 3%, from the USDC leg, fixed per offer when it is made), per-asset caps, two-step
ownership, and the Arc rules (USDC only through its ERC-20 interface, nothing payable, no transfers to
`address(0)`, expiry uses `block.timestamp > expiresAt`). No function lets the owner move anyone's funds.

## Checks

- The token must have code; fee-on-transfer and rebasing tokens are refused (the receiver's balance must rise by
  exactly the amount).
- Creating an offer needs the maker's balance and approval to be there now (stops spam offers).
- Can't make an offer to yourself or fill your own offer. Reentrancy guard on create and fill.
- Tokens that return nothing from `transferFrom` (USDT style) work.

## Tests

`test/foundry/ChordOTCv2.t.sol`: 23 unit and fuzz tests plus 2 invariants.

- Both directions, checking every balance and that the contract holds **nothing** afterwards.
- Maker moves funds or revokes approval after posting: the fill fails and the taker keeps everything.
- Blocklisted taker, blocklisted fee recipient (the owner redirects the fee, then fills work).
- Expiry (fine at exactly `expiresAt`), private taker, fill once only, own-offer fill, cancel rules.
- Fee-on-transfer token in both directions, fee fixed at creation, zero fee, caps, pause, admin limits,
  two-step ownership, constructor checks, native value refused, a re-entering token.
- Fuzz: any amounts and fee from 0 to 3% in both directions; value is conserved.
- **Invariants:** across random create/fill/cancel/move-away sequences (256 × 100 calls; 1,000 × 200 with
  `FOUNDRY_PROFILE=ci`), the contract never holds USDC, tokens or native value, and no value is created or lost.

`test/foundry/ChordOTCv2.fork.t.sol`: on a copy of **Arc mainnet** with the real USDC and EURC contracts, trades
both ways, a maker who spends their USDC after posting, and a USDC-blocklisted maker (the fill fails, nothing
moves, then it fills once they're unblocked). Runs in CI (the "Arc mainnet fork tests" job).

Also checked: compiled with EVM **paris**, no PUSH0. The site was run end to end in a browser against a local
chain: create (nothing left the maker's wallet), a stale offer greyed out as "Not fillable", fill (0.25% fee to
the treasury, contract at zero), the `/v1/` page, and phone width.

## Deploying

Same route as v1 (Remix + MetaMask, then the Safe). Do testnet first, then mainnet.

1. **Compile** in Remix: `contracts/ChordOTCv2.sol`, solc **0.8.24**, optimizer **on, 200**, EVM **paris**.
   (`remix-v2/ChordOTCv2_standard_input.json` has exactly these settings, for explorer verification.)
2. **Deploy** from Chord_Deployer: `ChordOTCv2(_usdc, _feeRecipient)` with
   `_usdc = 0x3600000000000000000000000000000000000000` (Arc USDC, same on testnet and mainnet) and
   `_feeRecipient = 0xc3FC2A5D1E7e2F5d597bE891e4BC36F117D9d4B6` (the Safe; on testnet use Chord_Deployer).
3. **Verify** on the Arc explorer with the standard JSON input.
4. **Settings from Chord_Deployer, before handing over** (mainnet):
   - `setFeeBps(0)` for the zero-fee promo (the 31 Dec reminder then has to call `setFeeBps(25)` on **v2**).
   - `setMaxOfferAmount` for the same 8 caps as v1 (table in MAINNET.md: USDC and EURC `1000000000`, WETH
     `500000000000000000`, cirBTC `2000000`, LINK `80000000000000000000`, VIRTUAL `1500000000000000000000`,
     ARGUS `135000000000000000000000`, ARCUS `20000000000000000000000000`).
5. **Hand ownership to the Safe:** Chord_Deployer `transferOwnership(Safe)`, then the Safe calls
   `acceptOwnership()` (Transaction Builder, 2 signatures). Check `owner()` shows the Safe.
6. **Pause new offers on v1** from the Safe: `setPaused(true)` on `0x112a…F097`. Existing v1 offers can still be
   filled or cancelled, and credits withdrawn, from `/v1/`.
7. **Smoke test:** offer 1 USDC for EURC from Chord_Deployer; check the USDC is still in the wallet; fill from
   Chord_Taker; check the balances, and that the contract holds nothing.
8. **Site:** put the addresses in `site/index.html` (`TESTNET_V2_ADDRESS`, `MAINNET_V2_ADDRESS`), then merge.

## v2.1

**Deployed on Arc mainnet (24 Nov 2026):** `0xcDAA3E1A661B59f7eA9d8804c971C12D2A0dE5c4`, from Chord_Deployer (block
32818489). Verified on explorer.arc.io (partial match: metadata hash only). Fee 0 (promo), the 8 beta caps set,

**Smoke test passed (24 Nov 2026, 08:11):** offer #1, Chord_Deployer gave 0.1 EURC for 0.11 USDC (maxFeeBps 0);
Chord_Taker filled it (tx `0x4625ec329434b8a66f928b47e0fa688ceda0cfe49ab10249bde8d66123634d63`). Two transfers, both
wallet to wallet; the contract holds nothing.

After the Olympix BugPoCer scan (19 Nov 2026), `contracts/ChordOTCv2.sol` on the `v2.1` branch adds:

- **Committed amounts (finding 5.1.1).** `committed[maker][asset]` is the total promised by a maker's Open offers
  on an asset. A new offer needs balance and approval for that total plus the new amount; fill and cancel release
  it. An approval given for one offer can no longer bring back an older offer that lost its backing. An expired
  offer stays committed until its maker cancels it.
- **Maker's maximum fee (5.2.1).** Both create functions take a last argument `uint16 maxFeeBps` and revert with
  "fee above your max" if the fee is higher. `OfferCreated` gains a final `uint16 feeBps` field.
- **Caps (5.2.2).** `maxOfferAmount` is a two-sided trade-size limit: it applies to both the asset given and the
  asset wanted (unchanged behaviour, corrected docs).
- **Taker's own offers stay backed (Proovant CHORD-02).** A fill reverts if it would leave the taker's own Open
  offers on the asset they pay with short of balance ("fill would leave your own open offers short of balance") or
  approval ("approve this fill on top of your own open offers"). The site already approves fills on top of your
  open offers; when v2.1 goes live, turn its balance warning in `doFill` into a hard stop, since the contract
  will refuse that fill anyway.
- The pre-audit review fixes F-01, F-05, F-06, F-21, F-22, F-25.

ABI changes for the site, Arc MCP and anything else that creates offers: the extra `maxFeeBps` argument and the
extra `feeBps` event field. Deploy the same way as v2 (Remix, then the Safe). Verification input:
`remix-v2/ChordOTCv2_1_standard_input.json` (same settings as v2; compiles with no errors, about 10 KB of bytecode).

**Don't pause v2 straight away.** Unlike v1, pausing v2 also stops fills of its open offers. Point the site at v2.1
first, let open v2 offers expire or be cancelled (default expiry is 7 days), then pause v2 from the Safe.
